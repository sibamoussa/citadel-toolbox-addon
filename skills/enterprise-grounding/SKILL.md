# Enterprise Grounding

Use approved enterprise knowledge before relying on general model knowledge.

## Instructions

1. Identify the entity, jurisdiction, topic, and effective date relevant to the request.
2. Use `tool_search` to discover enterprise knowledge capabilities when they are not already visible.
3. Search the entity knowledge index using the user's terminology and likely official synonyms.
4. Prefer currently effective, authoritative documents over summaries or older versions.
5. State when sources conflict, are outdated, or do not answer the question.
6. Cite the document title and available source URL or identifier for material claims.
7. Do not infer an entitlement, approval, legal conclusion, or policy exception that the source does not state.
8. Ask for clarification when the applicable entity, employee group, program, or jurisdiction is ambiguous.

## Output

Give the direct answer first, followed by concise supporting details and citations. Clearly label
uncertainty and the next official channel when evidence is insufficient.
